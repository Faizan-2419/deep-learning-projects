OUR APPROACH

From lengthy dialogue to meaningful clarity.

Sumora AI offers a focused way to summarize conversation-style text. Instead of manually identifying important information, users can generate a shorter version of their dialogue.

The application combines an AI summarization model with a clean, straightforward web interface. The result is a workflow designed around simplicity and readability.

A NOTE ON AI SUMMARIZATION

Technology is useful. Human judgment still matters.

AI-generated summaries are not guaranteed to be completely accurate. Important details may occasionally be omitted or misrepresented.

The current T5 model processes up to 512 input tokens, and longer conversations may be truncated. For important information, always compare the summary with the original conversation.
