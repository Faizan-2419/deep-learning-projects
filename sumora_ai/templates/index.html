GOOD TO KNOW

Curious?
We've got answers.

A few helpful details about what Sumora AI does and how to make the most of it.

What kind of text works best? +

Sumora AI is designed primarily for dialogue-style content, including discussions, chat exchanges, and conversational transcripts.

Do I need technical knowledge? +

No. You can simply paste a conversation into the workspace and generate a summary.

Can I summarize long conversations? +

You can enter longer conversations, but the current model processes a maximum of 512 input tokens. Text exceeding that limit is truncated, so important information near the end might not be considered.

Can I use the generated summary elsewhere? +

Yes. The workspace includes an option to copy your summary so you can paste it into your own notes or documents.

Are AI-generated summaries always accurate? +

Not necessarily. As with other generative AI systems, summaries can occasionally omit details or contain inaccuracies. Review important information against the original conversation.
